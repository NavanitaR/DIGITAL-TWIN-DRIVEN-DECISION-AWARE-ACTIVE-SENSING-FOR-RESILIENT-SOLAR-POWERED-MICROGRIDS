# Known limitations and acceptance status

This repository is a working software prototype with buildable device firmware and review-level electrical documentation. It does **not** satisfy physical commissioning acceptance or establish an assembly-safe power circuit.

- Hardware purchasing is provisional: no actual panel cold-Voc curve, 1S5Ah pack/PCM rating, charger assembly/NTC configuration, converter module rating or exact C3/display revision has been confirmed. Budget figures are engineering allowances, not verified live INR prices.
- Circuit drawings specify connection interfaces and protection requirements. Complete transistor-level charger/boost PCB layouts, final passive/fuse coordination, KiCad ERC and thermal/SOA validation remain unresolved. The supplied KiCad file is an integration connector/net map.
- Revision2 coordinates simultaneous operation from a common PV bus using existing charger DPM and MOSFET inhibit. Analog loop interaction, converter limits and all physical commissioning remain unverified. Controller/coil adapters are externally powered.
- Source relays have no wired auxiliary feedback. Source/bus voltage mismatch latches a gross actuation fault after2s, but cannot prove individual contact position or guarantee isolation of welded contacts. Independent emergency disconnect and reverse-feed protection are required.
- INA226 and DHT measurements have explicit invalid handling. DHT transactions are staggered but briefly blocking; the firmware is cooperative rather than fully interrupt-driven. Firmware watchdog/reset behaviour is compiled, not electrically tested.
- Sensor fault/health masks do not encode a complete diagnostic cause taxonomy. Low output cannot uniquely distinguish shade, disconnection and every short. No automatic reconnection follows a hard branch latch. A local USB service clear leaves the branch isolated; confirmed reconnect is separate.
- LoRa CRC and latest-sequence duplicate checks protect framing/idempotence only. There is no keyed RF authentication or robust multi-session replay protection. Bounded retries and airtime are implemented; range, collisions, ERP and legal equipment approval remain unverified.
- STM32 SOC uses configurable capacity and an uncalibrated50% initial state. The simplified twin uses a linear OCV approximation, ideal constant-power loads and fixed efficiencies. Runtime/error/uncertainty are estimates, not reference measurements. No trained fault classifier or unsupported AI claim is included.
- The simulation validates wire/framing hops but does not emulate the actual MQTT broker, RF propagation, UART interrupts or every MCU instruction. Hardware-mode MQTT accepts actual packets only. Browser QA may be unavailable in the tool environment; HTTP/build/component checks are reported separately.
- DTAV implements approximate EVSI, fresh requests, posterior reevaluation and safety-limited supervisory service commands. Hardware automatic service commands require `MICROGRID_DTAV_ARMED=1` after commissioning; default hardware operation permits measurement requests and deterministic local control. Simulation can evaluate the full request/action/response path. No novelty or universal improvement is claimed.
- TFT has seven implemented pages and confirmation controls, fixed hardware-safe priorities/thresholds and limited settings. Arbitrary persistent threshold/priority editing, configurable brightness, complete per-fault cause text and hardware charging-status GPIO are not implemented; they require purchased hardware/UI expansion. The frontend covers core live/simulation controls and charts but does not replace a laboratory SCADA security product.

These limits are explicit deviations from the broad master specification, not claimed completed features. See commissioning and validation documents for the remaining work and evidence.
